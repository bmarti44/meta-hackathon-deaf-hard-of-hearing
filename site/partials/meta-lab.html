Our model, checked by Meta's Muse · a listening test for a computer

Can a computer understand the voice better after our cleanup?

We took a person reading a book out loud and buried their voice in a crowd of other people talking. Then we cleaned it up with our model. Meta's Muse model listened to both and wrote down what it heard. Pick a recording to hear it and see what Muse wrote.

In the hardest recordings, where the crowd is louder than the voice, the cleaned audio sometimes confused Muse more than the raw one. That is the same wall the rest of this page is about.
