## 2. The retrospective audit estimands (M-P7-1, normative)

Unit: child (ears clustered within child). Index: each
decision origin $o \in$ {birth, 3, 6, 9, 12 mo} the child
historically reached before first CI surgery/activation
(or before 12 mo if no CI in window — see censoring).
At each origin the auditor (human, §7) assigns exactly
one R-P6-9 disposition using **only** record fields
timestamped $\le o$ (decision-time discipline, P6 §3
verbatim): genotype state at $o$ (R-P6-1/2 — a result
reported after $o$ is `pending` at $o$ even if the
sample pre-dates $o$; report date, not sample date, is
the information date, and both are extracted so the
choice is auditable), ABR study-ear ≥85 dB HL with
measurable response at $o$ (R-P6-3), age/cohort
actionability under the **dated source of record in
force for the audit** (R-P6-4/5 — see the anachronism
rule below), bilateral-CI status at $o$ (R-P6-6),
silent-state handling (R-P6-7, default frozen pending
HP-P6-1), and the never-delay construction (R-P6-8).

- **E1 — actionable-cell size.** For each origin $o$:
  $\hat\pi_o$ = (children whose disposition at $o$ is
  `referral-actionable`) / (P0 children reaching $o$
  uncensored), with exact (Clopper–Pearson) 95% CI;
  a zero numerator prints the inherited $3/N$ one-sided
  95% bound (P4/P6 verbatim). Printed **per origin**,
  never pooled across origins or across
  actionable/inactionable, and per R-P6-9 disposition
  cell with its denominator. Child-level secondary:
  proportion ever `referral-actionable` at ≥1 origin
  ≤12 mo, with the origin of first actionability.
  E1 is a size/precision estimand, not an effect:
  SONIX results, time-to-access under referral, and
  language outcomes are **outside** P7 entirely (D13
  preserved).
- **E2 — shadow attributable-delay distribution.**
  The audit cannot observe delay *caused by* a branch
  that did not exist; claiming otherwise would invent
  a counterfactual. P7 therefore defines the honest
  retrospective quantity: **slack** at first
  actionability. For each child ever confirmed-
  biallelic-P/LP before 12 mo, let $T^{*} =
  \max(\text{confirmation report date},\ \text{date the
  child reaches 9 mo})$ (R-P6-4 floor; cohort-open date
  enters only via the anachronism rule). Let $T_{CI}$ =
  date of the first CI surgery that the inspected
  source-of-record trial structure would conflict with
  (any-ear indicated CI on the frozen critical path,
  R-P6-8). Slack $S = T_{CI} - T^{*}$ in days.
  $S < 0$ ⇒ the child prints `never-delay-conflict` —
  branch actionability would have arrived only after
  the frozen CI it must never delay. Report: full
  empirical distribution of $S$ among confirmed
  children (median, IQR, min, and shares with
  $S \in \{<0,\ 0\text{–}7,\ 8\text{–}14,\ 15\text{–}30,\ >30\}$ d,
  mirroring the inherited 0/7/14/30 grid as *descriptive
  bins*, **not** as tolerance), plus E2's I1* form:
  count and share with $S<0$. **Zero tolerance is
  unchanged:** any prospective attributable day
  remains an I1* process FAIL; $S$ grades *risk*, it
  licenses no wait. Children with no CI date in record
  (no CI, CI after record close, loss) are censored at
  record close and reported as their own cell —
  censoring is never silently counted as slack.
- **E3 — classifiability (feasibility).** Share of
  child-origins where timestamps needed by R-P6-1…8
  are missing/undated in the source record
  (`unclassifiable`), by field (genotype report date,
  genotype state, per-frequency ABR by ear, CI dates
  by ear, diagnosis date). E3 is reported first: if
  E3 is large, E1/E2 print INSUFFICIENT regardless of
  their point values (kill arm §6.iii; threshold is a
  holder pin, HP-P7-1 — **no threshold number is
  asserted here**).
- **Anachronism rule (printed, load-bearing).**
  NCT07627971 did not exist during most historical
  records' windows. P7 is therefore a *counterfactual
  shadow* audit: R-P6-5 cohort/slot gating is applied
  under an explicitly named, dated assumption set —
  **(A-primary)** cohort state as in the inspected
  2026-11-03 source of record (Older 2–7 y cohort
  first, Younger 9 mo–<2 y second; see resweep §2 /
  resweep2 §2), treated as if in force at each
  historical origin; **(A-sensitivity)** cohorts
  ignored (age floor only). Both print; A-primary
  decides E1's headline cell. Results
  may never be quoted as "children who could have
  joined SONIX" — they estimate the branch's
  *steady-state* cell under today's inspected trial
  structure, nothing more. Era effects (genetic-test
  availability, turnaround, CI age norms changed over
  the record eras below) are a named bias (§4), handled
  by era stratification, never by pooling away.
- **Turnaround legs (descriptive, feeds U09).** Where
  dates exist: sample→report days, diagnosis→genotype-
  confirmation days, diagnosis→first-CI days, each as
  median+IQR by era and by genotype state. These are
  the first *measured* frozen-workup turnaround
  distributions in the record if the audit runs — they
  describe the baseline P4–P6 could only plan to
  measure. Yield (diagnoses/tested) is reported only
  as a denominator for disposition states, **never as
  an endpoint** (D6 verbatim).

## 4. Bias and validity threats (pre-registered readings)

- **Back-dating (the cardinal retrospective sin):**
  later-known genotype/outcome information must not
  leak into earlier origins. Mitigation: timestamp
  rule (§2), extractors blinded to post-$o$ fields per
  child-origin (truncated record views), audit of a
  20% dual-extraction subset (mirrors M-10's spirit;
  fraction inherited as PROPOSED-FROZEN, not ratified).
  A leak found in dual extraction = E3 event.
- **Era effects:** testing availability, turnaround,
  CI candidacy age, and EHDI timeliness (S2/S6) all
  moved across 2002–2026. Origins are stratified by
  record era (pre-specified by data source, e.g.
  birth-year bands pinned under HP-P7-3 before seeing
  dispositions); pooled E1 across eras is printed
  only beneath era-stratified cells, never instead.
- **Ascertainment:** centre/lab series (S5/S8)
  over-sample tested, implanted, insured, and
  followed children. G6 disparity reading: E1/E2 print
  by insurance/rurality/home-language where fields
  exist (S8 shows insurance alone moves medians by
  >1 y in that series — pooling would manufacture
  slack). State EHDI linkage (S1/S3) is the
  population sensitivity analysis, not a substitute.
- **Censoring/competing events:** death, move, loss,
  family decline of CI/testing, CI after 12 mo — each
  its own cell (E2 rule above). Children diagnosed
  after 12 mo (S8's median diagnosis was 15 mo in its
  era) contribute no origins and are printed as the
  **late-diagnosis cell** — a pathway finding in its
  own right, never dropped.
- **Anachronism (A-primary/A-sensitivity, §2):** SONIX
  structure projected backwards. Named, sensitivity-
  bounded, never quoted as historical eligibility.
- **Missing timestamps:** E3 governs; undated result
  = `pending` at every origin it cannot be placed
  before (conservative for E1, printed), with a
  sensitivity using sample date. No imputation of
  genotype state, ABR, or CI dates anywhere in P7.
