## Why this first

G3b (communication autonomy / family QoL) is BLOCKED for every
candidate (GOAL §4.2): no instrument + MCID may be scored until
stakeholders co-design them. Track-P promotion requires G3b
PASS, so **no win is possible while this is empty** — regardless
of device evidence.

## Deliverable (acceptance criteria)

1. Named instrument per window W1–W3 (or one instrument spanning
   them), administrable in signed/spoken/bimodal families.
2. Scoring rule + missing-data rule, pre-registered.
3. Window-specific MCID (superiority) and NI margin, with the
   elicitation method documented (who, how many, dissent kept).
4. Construct-validity + feasibility/pilot evidence summary.
5. Appendix A v2 G3b rows ready to paste (measure/instrument/
   unit/frozen ref/PASS delta/NI margin/min tier).

Not acceptable: picking a convenient questionnaire without
stakeholder decision rights; holder-only selection (§12 COI).
