### 3.1 PMID 41085057 — DB-OTO, NEJM 2026 (CHORD, NCT05788536)

Abstract inspected in full. Open-label single-group
first-in-human registrational study; children with *OTOF*
variants, profound deafness (average threshold >90 dB HL);
dual-AAV1 DB-OTO, **7.2×10¹² vg/ear**, one or both ears,
hair-cell-specific promoter. Primary endpoint: average
behavioral PTA ≤70 dB HL at week 24. **Results as stated:
12 children enrolled; 9/12 (75%; 95% CI 43–95) met both
the primary endpoint and the key secondary (click ABR
≤90 dB nHL) at week 24; 6 could hear soft speech without
assistive devices; 3 had average normal hearing
sensitivity; 67 adverse events occurred/worsened, none
leading to discontinuation.** Industry-funded (Regeneron;
allegiance consideration §5.2 at human extraction).
NCT05788536 registry record also inspected: RECRUITING,
est. N=36, primary completion 2029-02-26 (est.),
`hasResults: false` on ClinicalTrials.gov (journal
publication precedes registry results posting — noted,
not reconciled here).

**What this does and does not do here:** It is the

### 3.2 PMID 40690227 — GT vs CI cohort, JAMA Neurol 2025 (codex §2, U1)

Abstract inspected in full (codex inspected the publisher
full text; human dual extraction still PENDING — codex
§4 rank 3). Nonblind single-centre cohort (Dec 2022–Nov
2024), congenital severe-to-complete loss, ages 1–18 y,
matched on deafness duration/thresholds/baseline speech;
**11 GT** (mean age 3.7 y) vs **61 CI** (mean 1.9 y) of
1568 screened. As stated: GT ABR thresholds restored
>95 → 54.8 (SD 15.9) dB nHL in 9 GT patients at 12 mo;
GT-only > CI on IT-MAIS/MAIS at 6 and 12 mo; bimodal

tune rate only, among the stated comparisons.

**Disposition (codex §2, confirmed at abstract level):**

**unilateral** CI, so it cannot pass the frozen
**bilateral** G2 contrast; nonblind, single-centre,
age-imbalanced. A genuine comparative research lead —
DESIGN input to pathway/comparator definitions, **not**
an efficacy result attributable to Ga/P4/P5/P6, and not
validation.
