## Per-gate evidence

**G1 — NA-prereg.**

**Best available synthesis — Fitzpatrick et al. 2016
(PMID 26684476, abstract inspected 2026-11-02; frozen S-H
systematic-review subset, 4 hits, 1 on-question).** Systematic
review (Medline/Embase/CINAHL 1995–Jun 2013 + grey literature,
dual screening/extraction, GRADE applied by the review): 11
cohort studies of early sign+oral vs oral-only intervention, 8
in severe–profound hearing loss with CIs; language the commonest
outcome, speech and speech perception also reported; measures
heterogeneous, interventions sometimes unclear. Authors'
conclusion, which this score adopts rather than re-derives:
*"Very limited, and hence insufficient, high-quality evidence
exists to determine whether sign language in combination with
oral language is more effective than oral language therapy
alone."* For this extraction the record is abstract-level and
its primaries were not re-extracted (protocol §4: extract the
primary or mark numbers UNVERIFIABLE — the abstract reports no
pooled effect, signed-stratum NI margin, or child N, so none is
invented). GRADE for every H cell it touches: VeryLow →
INSUFFICIENT. Note what this review is *not*: its comparator is
oral-only therapy, not the frozen EI mix as actually received
(P07, family-chosen modality), and its cohorts predate H's
dose-specified encouragement design. It bounds the prior
literature; it does not test H.

**Motivating reviews — framing only (REVIEW tier).** Kral &
Sharma 2023 (PMID 36990952) argue crossmodal reorganisation is
compensatory/reversible and that "no sign before CI" advice
needs modification — a crossmodal-plasticity *review* (Step 0
Ch3, partial-PDF pp.377–378 caveat B2), establishing no causal
H effect and no sensitive-period gradient (the 2012 review is
that anchor, also a review). Kral et al. 2025 (PMID 40907462)
*propose* first-nine-months sensorimotor contingency while
citing a ~3-year critical period in the same abstract (Ch4) —
W0 benefit stays a hypothesis. Sharma P1 data (Ch5) begin at
1.3 y and are mechanism only. None of these can PASS or kill H;
H must test auditory non-harm, not assume it (candidate-H §2).

**G3a signed — INSUFFICIENT; measurability PROVISIONAL-SIGNAL.**
ASL-CDI 2.0 (Caselli et al. 2020, PMID 32180180 [App. A N7],
abstract re-inspected): normed receptive+expressive vocabulary
+ Gestures/Phrases, N=120 deaf children *of deaf parents*,
9–73 mo. Two travelling caveats: most P0 children have hearing
parents with later/variable ASL exposure, so scores measure
vocabulary *given exposure* (dose must be reported or a low
score is an exposure finding, not a device/pathway finding);
and beyond 73 mo (late W3) no inspected ASL-normed battery
exists, so late-W3 signed cells are unscorable. Non-ASL sign
languages need their own instruments (UNPINNABLE). Crucially,
no inspected study reports H's estimand: encouragement ITT, or
encouragement-as-instrument complier effect of signed-exposure
dose, on ASL-CDI against the −0.2 SD NI margin / +0.4 SD PASS
conventions (themselves PENDING-STAKEHOLDER). Observational
dose–response with the §4.2 adjustment set would still be
non-causal sensitivity by pre-registered rule.

**G3a spoken & bimodal — INSUFFICIENT.** Spoken instruments
uncited (U11) + frozen SS blank (U10) block any spoken PASS;
no pooled SD across norms, ever. The bimodal hierarchy
(highest-modality primary, total-conceptual-vocabulary NI
co-check, single-modality scores always reported) is a
PENDING-STAKEHOLDER convention with zero H data behind it.

**G2 post-CI auditory — INSUFFICIENT (kill arm b input).**

## What would change this score

The pre-registered encouragement cohort itself: randomise the
*offer* (funded Deaf-mentor access + scheduling/transport
support, contact-time-matched spoken-EI attention control),
never modality; report ITT encouragement effects and IV dose
effects with first-stage strength, exclusion/monotonicity
falsification checks, and negative controls; dose in provider
hrs/wk + caregiver hrs/day (time-varying); ASL-CDI 2.0 (≤73 mo,
exposure-reported) + cited spoken battery + bimodal hierarchy;
same-test post-CI G2 NI; per-child CI-delay accounting under
treatment-policy — all vs the P07 frozen EI mix measured
concurrently, with human dual extraction and Deaf-community
review of materials. An ASL-normed battery beyond 73 mo is also
owed. Until then H is a well-posed, ethically careful question
with an insufficient evidence base — which is exactly what the
best inspected synthesis already said in 2016.
