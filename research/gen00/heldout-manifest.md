## 1. Partition rules (frozen)

1. **By study, never by rows.** All reports/arms/windows of
   one study or cohort sit on one side together (GOAL §5.2).
   Splitting a study's rows across design/validation is
   forbidden. Registry record + label + journal report of one
   trial (e.g. CHORD) = one study unit.
2. **By date.** Design: publication/registration/inspection
   date **≤2026-11-02** *and* inspected in Step 0/1 (listed in
   §2). Late-found records dated ≤2026-11-02 discovered in
   Step 3 join Design as "late-found design" (protocol §3).
   Validation-eligible on date: first published/registered
   **after 2026-11-02**.
3. **By independence.** A post-freeze study from the *same*
   inventor lab extending its own design-set claim is
   "temporal validation (same-lab)" — counts for temporal
   checking but does **not** satisfy the non-inventor
   replication requirement for G1 PASS or the allegiance rule
   (protocol §6). Pre-freeze independent-lab studies already
   in §2 stay Design (they were seen); their independence is
   still credited inside GRADE, but they are not re-labelled
   Validation.
4. **By species.** A claim designed on mouse evidence needs a
   **non-mouse check** (other animal species or human) on the
   validation side where available (GOAL §5.2); analogously,
   single-species animal claims (gerbil, guinea pig, cat)
   headline their species and need cross-species or human
   corroboration before tier upgrade arguments. Species is
   recorded per study in §2 for exactly this check.
5. **Extraction upgrade ≠ validation.** Human full-text
   re-extraction of a §2 study (e.g. USPI PDF re-read, NEJM
   full text) raises its evidence level *within Design*. It
   never moves the study to Validation.
6. **No retroactive moves.** List membership changes only by
   dated amendment appending new studies (§5); §2 entries are
   never deleted or re-sided. Corrections = new versions.
7. **Opening rule.** Validation evidence is "opened" (extracted
   for scoring) only after this manifest + `freeze-checklist.md`
   are committed. Any validation study read before that commit
   by the scoring team joins Design with the read date logged
   (contamination rule — when in doubt, demote to Design).

and must be printed in Step 3 reports until entries accrue by
amendment. A study enters Validation only if **all** hold:

   dual extraction before any PASS use — protocol §§6/8).
3. Logged here by amendment (§5) with study unit, date,
   species, lab independence vs the design claim it checks,

   *before* its results influence any score revision.

   (rule 4). For inventor-lab claims: non-inventor lab for
   replication credit (rule 3); same-lab later studies are
   "temporal validation (same-lab)", a weaker sub-label.
