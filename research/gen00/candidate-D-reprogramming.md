## 3. The single advance criterion (verbatim) & what counts as meeting it

**GOAL §8D (verbatim):** *“replicated threshold restoration with
cochlear-identity, synapsed cells in a mature deafened large-animal
cochlea.”* Until met (by anyone), D = literature watch only. Pre-committed
reading — all five conjuncts required; failure of any one = criterion unmet:

1. **Replicated:** ≥2 independent groups (non-originating-lab replication,
   §5.2 allegiance rule) or one group + independent held-out replication
   cohort, animal-level denominators published.
2. **Threshold restoration:** ABR (and/or DPOAE where OHC claim) threshold
   shift toward normal with effect size + N + CI from human-extracted full
   text — marker+ cells without thresholds (current state, D4a/D4c) do not
   count. No numeric dB threshold is invented here; the numeric restoration
   margin must be frozen *before* the qualifying study is scored, from its
   own deafened-control distribution.
3. **Cochlear-identity:** converted cells in the organ of Corti (not ectopic
   under/adjacent), cochlear (not vestibular) marker/identity profile,
   hair-bundle morphology reported.
4. **Synapsed:** pre/post-synaptic pairing with SGN neurites quantified
   (counts per converted cell), SGN survival reported.
5. **Mature deafened large-animal:** adult, deafened (method + deafness
   duration stated), large-animal cochlea (species justified as
   translationally relevant in size/delivery); neonatal mouse and organoid
   results are T1 hypothesis-generation forever for this criterion.

Supporting-cell depletion, off-fate rate, durability, and tumour
surveillance must accompany any qualifying report or it scores INSUFFICIENT
evidence for advancement even if thresholds move.
