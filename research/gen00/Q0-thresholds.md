## 1. Q0 decomposed (label ≠ biology, always separate)

**Q0:** Does early cochlear implantation *biologically*
foreclose later same-ear AAV gene/cell therapy, beyond the
current regulatory exclusion?

Three columns in every Q0 table — never merged:

| Column | Content | State from seen evidence (not a score) |
|---|---|---|
| **Q0-L (label/regulatory)** | Trial/label eligibility as written. Otarmeni USPI §1: biallelic *OTOF*, preserved outer-hair-cell function, **no prior CI in the same ear** (Step 0 O1b, verified authoritative record; "same ear" is label wording) | Established label fact about the *studied* population. Says nothing about biology in unstudied post-CI ears — in either direction |
| **Q0-P (patency/access)** | Post-CI scala patency: can a later infusion physically access the scala? Operational definition in §2.1 | No inspected source reports a patency/cannulability % by time-since-implant (Step 0 E4a; Appendix A v1 §7.1 UNPINNABLE) |
| **Q0-A (post-CI AAV efficacy)** | If accessed, does AAV delivered *after* an established implant (+ fibrosis course) transduce targets and restore function vs an unimplanted comparator? Operational definition in §2.2 | No direct inspected evidence either way (Step 0 E4b post-skeptic). AAV-before/concurrent-with-implant studies do not answer Q0-A |

Tissue-response facts that *are* seen (abstract-level, small
adult older-device series — presence near-universal, volume/
distribution variable; Step 0 E4a) motivate Q0-P but do not
fill it: fibrosis/new-bone presence ≠ quantified patency, and
hearing-preservation rates ≠ patency (E4c correction).
Contralateral-ear strategies, explants, and atraumatic
surgery (GOAL §1.3 counterexamples) are *mitigations* scored
under Families G/A, not substitutes for same-ear Q0-P/Q0-A.

> **sensitivity-only screening outputs** (protocol §6

### 2.1 Q0-P — scala patency %

**Estimand.** Among implanted ears meeting a pre-stated
cohort definition (age at implant, array type/era, insertion
route incl. round-window vs cochleostomy, time-since-implant
band — all extracted, never pooled silently), the proportion
whose target scala (scala tympani unless otherwise
pre-stated) is **patent/cannulable for later infusion** under
a pre-stated access definition (imaging-based, histology-
based, or surgical cannulation — definition + grader +
blinding recorded per study). Reported as **n/N (%)** with
denominator = all implanted ears in the cohort (treatment-
policy spirit: explanted/failed/ungradeable ears stay in N
with reason; complete-case % reported as sensitivity only).
Unit: ear clustered by child/animal (protocol §2).

**Thresholds (CONVENTION).**

| Zone | Patency point estimate | Meaning for Q0-P |
|---|---|---|
| **HIGH (access usually retained)** | **≥80%** | Q0-P component consistent with "sequencing usually possible" |
| **INTERMEDIATE (access often lost)** | **>50% to <80%** | Partial foreclosure: a substantial minority lose same-ear access |
| **LOW (access usually lost)** | **≤50%** | Q0-P component consistent with material foreclosure |

*Justification (convention, pending ratification).* 80% =
"at most 1 in 5 children lose the same-ear option" — chosen
as the loosest cut at which telling families "CI today usually
keeps tomorrow's same-ear option open" remains honest without
further qualification. 50% = coin-flip: below it, "preserved
option" language is misleading by definition. Neither cut is
an MCID, a literature value, or a clinical guideline; both
are value-laden communication/decision conventions that
otology + stakeholder reviewers may tighten or loosen at
ratification. **Mandatory sensitivities:** re-report the Q0-P
zone at HIGH cuts **70%** and **90%** alongside 80% in every
headline. Where a 95% interval is extractable, report whether
it crosses the zone boundary (crossing = zone flagged
*uncertain*, GRADE precision downgrade considered — the point
estimate still determines the nominal zone; no CI-based zone
hopping).

*Time rule.* Patency is meaningless without time-since-
implant. Primary Q0-P read: the **longest pre-stated
time band available at ≥Moderate GRADE** (early patency that
later ossifies must not headline). Bands extracted as
reported; cross-band pooling forbidden.

### 2.2 Q0-A — post-CI AAV efficacy (relative retention)

**Estimand.** In a study where AAV is delivered **after an
established implant** (implant in situ through a pre-stated
fibrosis/healing course — duration + deafening model
extracted; AAV-before/concurrent designs excluded from Q0-A
per protocol §3 S-Q0b), efficacy **relative to a same-study
unimplanted (or sham-implanted, stated) comparator** receiving
the same capsid/promoter/transgene, dose (vg/ear + volume),
and route. Two co-reported readouts:

- **Q0-A1 (transduction):** % target cells transduced (cell
  type named; counting method + blinding extracted) in
  post-CI ears ÷ % in comparator ears = **retention ratio**.
- **Q0-A2 (function):** hearing-threshold outcome (ABR/PTA
  improvement vs pre-AAV baseline, frequencies stated) in
  post-CI ears vs comparator — reported as **difference in
  improvement (dB)**; responder % (e.g. reaching a pre-stated
  PTA cut) co-reported with n/N if the study defines one.
  No cross-study dB pooling across different tests/models.

**Thresholds (CONVENTION).**

| Zone | Q0-A1 retention ratio | Q0-A2 functional difference* | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RETAINED** | **≥0.80** | Post-CI improvement within **10 dB** of comparator improvement | Post-CI delivery substantially preserves efficacy |
| **PARTIAL** | **>0.50 to <0.80** | 10–20 dB worse than comparator (or A1/A2 zones disagree) | Efficacy degraded but not abolished |
| **LOST** | **≤0.50** | >20 dB worse, or no threshold improvement where comparator improves | Material efficacy foreclosure |

\* Q0-A zone requires **both** A1 and A2 in the same zone for
RETAINED or LOST; disagreement = **PARTIAL** with the
discordance headlined (transduction without function, or
vice versa, is itself the finding). If only one readout
exists, Q0-A is at most PARTIAL-eligible and the missing
readout is named.

*Justification (convention, pending ratification).* 0.80
mirrors Q0-P's 80% "usually retained" communication standard
so the two Q0 components use one honesty ruler; 0.50 = half
the biological effect gone. The 10 dB / 20 dB functional
bands are *decision conventions* chosen because 10 dB is the
smallest threshold difference the ratifying panel is asked to
consider clearly beyond typical test–retest noise for
threshold audiometry/ABR steps, and 20 dB is twice that —
**this is a proposal for expert ratification or replacement
with instrument-specific margins, not a claim that any seen
source validates 10/20 dB as an MCID in P0.** No 3 dB G2-style
borrowing: Q0-A2 concerns AAV hearing restoration, not CI
SRT. **Mandatory sensitivities:** retention cuts **0.70** and
**0.90**; functional bands **5 dB** and **15 dB** in place of
10 dB. Absolute values (both arms' % and dB, with n/N) always
printed next to ratios/differences — a ratio over a near-zero
comparator effect is flagged *unstable* and cannot yield
RETAINED.

*Immunity caveat.* Q0-A concerns first post-CI AAV exposure.
Anti-capsid immunity / re-dosing is the separate G4-AAV
sub-gate (presumptive FAIL, protocol §6); a RETAINED Q0-A
does not satisfy G4-AAV.

### 2.3 Minimum evidence for any non-UNDECIDABLE Q0 verdict

All must hold (else §4 UNDECIDABLE):

1. **Tier:** Q0-P: human imaging/histology **or** T2
   mature/deafened animal with implanted-course patency
   grading (T0/T1 cannot decide patency). Q0-A: **T2 minimum**
   (mature/deafened animal, established-implant design);
   neonatal/small-animal-only or AAV-first designs cannot
   decide Q0-A. Human post-CI AAV data (T4) would supersede
   animal reads if ever inspected — none is asserted to exist.
2. **GRADE ≥Moderate** for the deciding component(s) after
   RoB (SYRCLE/ROBINS-I domains per protocol §5), allegiance
   downgrade, directness (adult archival / otosclerosis-only /
   older-device evidence downgraded for P0 transport — cf.
   Step 0 E4a corrections), and precision. Low = provisional
   Q0 signal (reported, verdict stays UNDECIDABLE-provisional,
   §4). Very low / high RoB = INSUFFICIENT input.
3. **Human dual extraction** completed for deciding rows
   (protocol §8). Abstract-only rows cap Q0 at provisional.
4. **Denominators:** n/N extractable for patency; per-arm N
   for Q0-A. "N not in abstract/record" = deciding use
   forbidden until full-text extraction supplies it.
5. **Independence:** at least one deciding input from the
   held-out/validation side *or* a non-inventor independent
   lab (`heldout-manifest.md`). A verdict resting solely on
   design evidence already seen at pre-registration must be
   labelled **design-only** and cannot exceed provisional —
   this is the price of the exploratory-after-seen header.

## 3. Composite Q0 decision rule

| Q0-P zone | Q0-A zone | Composite verdict | Project consequence (GOAL §1.3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| HIGH (≥80%) | RETAINED (≥0.80 + ≤10 dB) | **Q0-NO-FORECLOSURE:** sequencing solves the same-ear dilemma at the stated conventions | Pivot weight to optimizing sequencing (Family G) + evolving standard; preservation-first framing narrowed and said so plainly. Label Q0-L still governs *today's* eligibility until regulators/trials change it — biology ≠ permission |
| LOW (≤50%) **or** LOST (≤0.50 / >20 dB) (either component, other component not HIGH/RETAINED at ≥Moderate) | — | **Q0-FORECLOSURE:** early same-ear CI materially forecloses later same-ear AAV at the stated conventions | Preservation/sequencing rationale (G pre-CI triage, G4 veto weight) supported *as a premise* — each candidate still scored cell-by-cell; Q0 alone promotes nobody (G3b blocked regardless) |
| Any INTERMEDIATE/PARTIAL, or HIGH-with-PARTIAL, or components discordant | — | **Q0-PARTIAL:** foreclosure is real for a substantial subgroup / partially degraded | Stratum-specific follow-up (time band, array/route, anatomy) ranked as promotion-blocking gap; Family G scoring must use the partial numbers, not a binary story |
| Evidence below §2.3 minima for either component needed to place it | — | **Q0-UNDECIDABLE** (§4) | See §4 — no premise claimed in either direction |

Sensitivities (70/90%, 0.70/0.90, 5/15 dB) are applied to the
composite as well: if the composite flips under any mandatory
sensitivity, the headline reads e.g. "Q0-PARTIAL (NO-
FORECLOSURE at 70% sensitivity)" — flips are headlined, never
footnoted. Decision-time discipline applies: a patency rate
measured years post-implant informs the *future-therapy*
branch of a pathway decision; it is never back-dated as
information available at implant decision time beyond what
was knowable then.

## 4. UNDECIDABLE — defined outcome (not a failure mode)

**Definition.** Q0 is **UNDECIDABLE** when, after the frozen
Step 3 sweep (protocol §3 strings S-Q0a/S-Q0b + registries),
either Q0-P or Q0-A lacks evidence meeting §2.3 minima to
place it in a §2 zone. Sub-labels (exactly one, plus named
missing inputs):

- **UNDECIDABLE-no-data:** no eligible study found (bounded-
  search wording + date + registries). Absence of evidence,
  stated as such.
- **UNDECIDABLE-below-tier:** only T0/T1, AAV-first-sequence,
  or otherwise ineligible designs found.
- **UNDECIDABLE-below-GRADE:** eligible designs exist but all
  Low/Very-low or high-RoB after downgrades (incl. abstract-
  only / human-extraction-pending caps). If point estimates
  exist at Low GRADE, they are reported as *provisional Q0
  signals* with zones shown in brackets — the verdict word
  remains UNDECIDABLE.
- **UNDECIDABLE-unratified:** evidence meets §2.3 but §2
  thresholds were never human-ratified by Step 3 close —
  verdict reported as sensitivity-only under proposed
  conventions, freeze-grade verdict withheld.

**Consequences of UNDECIDABLE (binding).**

1. The preservation dilemma remains a **question, not a
   premise** (GOAL §1.3). Neither "CI forecloses gene
   therapy" nor "sequencing easily solves it" may be asserted
   anywhere in Gen 0 outputs. Family G may claim only what its
   own measured cells support (time-to-access, triage
   accuracy, label-eligible ears reached pre-CI) — never
   "preserved biological option" as an established benefit,
   and never penalized as if foreclosure were disproven.
2. All Q0-dependent gate-cells (G4 scala-patency component;
   any G claim of option-preservation) read **INSUFFICIENT**.
   Q0-L (label exclusion) remains separately reportable as a
   regulatory fact.
3. UNDECIDABLE triggers a named, ranked gap entry (GOAL §7
   rule 4): the Step 3 report must specify the study design(s)
   that *could* decide Q0 (e.g. established-implant animal
   AAV study per §2.2 estimand; prospective patency imaging/
   surgical series per §2.1 estimand) as prioritized
   study-design handoff candidates (GOAL §5.1 deliverable) —
   designs, not experiments run here (paper-only).
4. UNDECIDABLE does not block the rest of Gen 0 screening
   (G1 head-to-head, FMEA/timeline FAILs, within-study deltas
   proceed per Step 1 §6) and does not count as a dry round
   by itself; stopping rules stay as in GOAL §7 rule 5.
5. Re-opening: Q0 is re-scored only when new evidence meeting
   §2.3 appears (validation side) or thresholds are ratified/
   replaced by re-freeze. Re-wording the same evidence never
   re-opens it (`seen`-set discipline).

*Expectation note (not a verdict).* Given the seen evidence
(E4a/E4b voids), UNDECIDABLE-no-data or -below-GRADE is the
anticipated Step 3 outcome. Pre-registering thresholds anyway
is the point: whatever Step 3 finds is placed against numbers
fixed before scoring, and a future decider study can be
designed directly against §§2.1–2.2 estimands.
