## 2. Population, comparator, estimand, unit of analysis

**Population P0 (GOAL §3.1; Step 1 proposed pin P01–P03).**
Live-born infants in the **United States, birth year 2022**
(changing year requires re-extraction, never silent drift)

## 4. Inclusion / exclusion (frozen)

Apply at title/abstract, then at full-record inspection.
Flow (included/excluded with reasons) is published (GOAL §9).

**Include** (any one, if it maps to ≥1 pre-registered
gate-cell, Q0 component, or Appendix B field):

1. Human, animal, bench, or in-silico primary studies of a
   Family A–H mechanism, its comparator, or frozen bilateral
   eCI, reporting a gate-relevant outcome with an extractable
   N (or explicitly reporting that N is absent — the absence
   itself is extracted).
2. Systematic reviews / meta-analyses of the above (primary
   studies de-duplicated against (1); never double-counted).
3. Trial-registry records and FDA labels/decisions for the
   above (status/eligibility evidence; efficacy only if
   results are posted/inspected).
4. Human temporal-bone histopathology, imaging, explant/
   revision series, and established-implant animal studies
   bearing on Q0 (`Q0-thresholds.md` names eligible types).
5. Population/registry sources for P0 denominators, etiologic/
   anatomic fractions, and per-harm rates (Appendix D / G5 /
   G6), with jurisdiction, era, and ascertainment stated.

**Exclude** (logged with reason; excluded ≠ hidden):

1. Records with no gate-cell mapping (e.g. unrelated-organ
   AAV-BDNF work) — neighbouring mechanisms are named as
   non-equivalent, never pooled (cf. Step 0 D3c neighbours).
2. Secondary news/trade/press summaries as *evidence* (usable
   only as pointers to a primary record; cf. Step 0 O3b, D2b).
3. Reviews cited for numeric effects that belong to their
   primaries (extract the primary or mark the number
   UNVERIFIABLE; narrative reviews may support framing only,
   at review tier — e.g. Kral reviews, Step 0 Ch3/Ch4).
4. Unverifiable-as-cited references ("Liu 2025", "Qiao 2024"
   in GOAL shorthand; Step 0 D4a/D4b): deleted as citations;
   nearest fully-specified records (e.g. Liu 2024) are
   screened on their own merits, never as proxies.
5. Conductive/mixed-loss-only, unilateral-only (unless the
   gate-cell is explicitly ear-level and clustering is
   handled), and adult-acquired-loss-only records *for P0
   outcome claims* — they may still enter as tier-discounted
   proxy/mechanism evidence under §7, labelled as such.
6. Duplicate reports of the same study/cohort (link, extract
   once, cite all report IDs; unit-of-analysis is the study
   for held-out purposes — never split by rows within a study).

No record is excluded for direction of result. Null and
harm results are extracted with the same fields as positive
results (terminal accounting, GOAL §6: explants, failures,
vestibular/meningitis events, deaths, loss to follow-up stay
in denominators).

## 5. Risk of bias (tool by design, GOAL §5.2)

Every extracted study that could support a PASS or FAIL gets
a risk-of-bias (RoB) assessment with the tool matching its
design. "No RoB assessment" = cannot PASS (see §6 map).

| Design | Tool | Notes for this project |
|---|---|---|
| Randomized trial (human) | **RoB 2** | Per-outcome; blinding of outcome assessors stated (device trials often unblindable — assess measurement bias directly) |
| Non-randomized comparative (human) | **ROBINS-I** | Confounding set pre-stated per gate (G3: modality, home language, SES proxy, EI dose, additional disabilities; family-chosen modality is never "adjusted into" causality for H — GOAL §4.2/§8H identification strategy required) |
| Animal intervention study | **SYRCLE** | Randomization, allocation concealment, blinded outcome assessment, attrition by animal; deafening method/duration and age/maturity extracted (neonatal vs mature/deafened drives tier, §7) |
| Single-arm / case series / histopathology series | ROBINS-I domains where applicable + explicit selection/ascertainment statement | Small archival series (cf. Step 0 E4a: 17–28 bones, adult, older-device) can establish *presence* of tissue response; volume/distribution and infant transportability stay downgraded |
| Diagnostic-yield / pathway study (G) | ROBINS-I (comparative claims) + pre-stated spectrum/verification statement | Yield % without decision-time denominator + turnaround is not pathway evidence |
| In-silico / bench (T0) | No RoB tool — reproducibility checklist instead | Model+version+parameters+seeds; comparator device named (degraded comparators flagged, cf. Step 0 Ch2b); bench aging claims need N devices + failure definition or they stay pointers (Step 0 D2b) |
| Systematic review/meta-analysis | Assess the review's own RoB methods + extract primaries | Review verdict never substitutes for primary RoB on load-bearing cells |

Registry/label records are not RoB-scored; they are verified
as records (exact wording + locator) and their *underlying
studies* are RoB-scored separately. Abstract-level inspection
caps RoB at "provisional": domains invisible in an abstract
(per-arm N, attrition, definitions) are recorded as
UNVERIFIED, and the cell cannot exceed the §6 abstract cap
until full-text human extraction.

## 6. GRADE→score map + allegiance rule (binding, GOAL §5.2)

Grading: adapted GRADE per gate-cell (High / Moderate / Low /
Very low), integrating RoB (§5), tier (§7), directness to P0/
window/stratum, precision (N + interval where available),
consistency, and publication-bias check (§7).

| GRADE (after all downgrades) | Score consequence |
|---|---|
| **High or Moderate** AND Appendix A minimum tier met | Eligible for **PASS** (if effect meets Appendix A delta in decision-time analysis at primary + stress scenarios) or **FAIL** (if effect breaches NI/veto) |
| **Low** | **Provisional signal only** — reported, ranked, never PASS. Headlines must carry "Low GRADE — provisional signal" |
| **Very low**, or **high RoB** on a load-bearing domain, or below minimum tier, or missing | **INSUFFICIENT** — never PASS, never judge-imputed |

Additional binding rules:

- **Allegiance rule.** Inventor-lab single-source claims
  (device/program-originating groups; Step 0 names Moser-group,
  ANI-consortium, UNSW, Groves-lab single-source claims as
  downgrade targets) start **one GRADE level lower**. **G1
  signals require non-inventor replication to PASS.** Lab
  identity is extracted for every T0–T3 claim.
- **Abstract cap.** Evidence inspected only at abstract level
  (the state of most Step 0 "VERIFIED (abstract-level)" rows
  and Appendix A N1–N8) is PROVISIONAL: it may support a
  provisional signal or a completeness FAIL, but a numeric
  PASS requires full-text human dual extraction (§8). This
  cap is the Step 1 A4 governance applied prospectively.
- **Human-extraction cap.** No agent-pre-extracted row is
  gate-deciding until independent human verification (§8).
  Agent-only verification — including skeptic/synthesis passes
  — is circular for gate-deciding purposes (Step 0 header).
- **G3b override.** Regardless of GRADE/tier, every G3b cell
  is **INSUFFICIENT** at v1 (header). No GRADE language may
  soften this (never "G3b provisional pass").
- **G4-AAV override.** Any AAV-using challenger is a
  presumptive G4 **FAIL** until capsid-switch / non-viral
  re-dosing or post-exposure later-AAV efficacy is shown
  (GOAL §4.2). Anti-capsid immunity is explicitly scored
  (Appendix C row required).
- **Convention-threshold governance (Step 1 A4/V-C).**
  PASS/FAIL verdicts that depend on unratified conventions
  (G1 "8" ruler, G2/G3a deltas, G6 caps, Q0 thresholds) must
  be reported with the convention named and sensitivities
  shown. Until holder + co-approver freeze ratifies them
  (`freeze-checklist.md`), such verdicts are
  **sensitivity-only screening outputs**, not freeze-grade
  passes. G1 additionally requires the head-to-head bar
  against a named frozen array measured in the same study/
  model (Appendix A v1 §3.2) — a ratio to the "8" convention
  alone never passes; verdicts are reported at anchors
  4/10/16 as well as 8, in the headline.

## 7. Tiers, proxy chain, multiplicity, held-out rule

**Tiers (GOAL §5.3).** T0 in-silico/bench/in-vitro (mechanism
plausibility; never G2/G3 pass) · T1 neonatal small-animal
(hypothesis generation; heavily discounted G1/G4 signals
only) · T2 mature/deafened animal incl. large-animal where
relevant (G1/G4/G5 provisional; required before Track R
advancement) · T3 adult human incl. normal-hearing vocoder
(G1/G2 proxies; vocoder ≠ infant biology) · T4 pediatric
human, age-matched (required for any infant promotion claim).
Minimum tier per gate-cell is in Appendix A v1 §2. Reconciled
rule: **G3a PASS requires T4**; T3 + validated chain yields at
most a provisional signal.

**Infant proxy chain (each arrow discounted & stated):**
animal neurophysiology → adult psychophysics → infant
CAEP/behavioural (P1, VRA, IT-MAIS) → W2/W3 language.
Surrogates (P1 latency, fNIRS cross-modal index, modelled
channel count) count toward G3 only if pre-stated validation
criteria (association + mediation evidence) are met;
otherwise mechanism-only. P1/fNIRS never sole G3 passes.
Each candidate shows its early mechanism measure (W0/W1)
*and* downstream outcome (W2/W3) from linked cohorts, or
states the mediation assumption as UNRESOLVED (developmental
causal-chain requirement, GOAL §5.3).

**Multiplicity (GOAL §5.2).** The searched-family log (every
candidate × gate × window × stratum scored, including all
INSUFFICIENT cells) is published with Step 3 results. Formal
family-wise control (Holm or hierarchical gatekeeping, stated
in the analysis file before running) applies *only* to
quantitative meta-analytic sub-claims with pre-listed primary
comparisons. Qualitative gate scores use the log to discount
narrative confidence, labelled honestly as such.
Publication bias: trial-registry + grey-literature search
(§3); funnel/Egger only where ≥10 studies.

**Held-out rule (GOAL §5.2; manifest = `heldout-manifest.md`).**
Partition by **study** (never by rows within a study) and by
**date** (design ≤2026-11-02; validation = later and/or
independent labs) and by **species** (mouse-designed claim
needs a non-mouse check where available). Hypotheses designed
on earlier literature are checked against later/independent
evidence (temporal validation). The partition manifest is
committed with this protocol, before Step 3 scoring and
before validation evidence is opened (GOAL §7 rule 7).
Re-extracting the full text of a design study upgrades its
extraction level; it does **not** move it to validation.

**Fitness / ranking (for reporting order only — promotion is
gate-based).** Lexicographic Track P order per GOAL §7
(fewest FAILs with G4/G5 veto demoted last → fewest
INSUFFICIENT → G2 gain → G3a gain → G3b verdict → device G1
→ lowest burden), with rank-sensitivity to order reported
and the disclosed bias that fewest-INSUFFICIENT favours
studied incumbents (evidence-completeness sensitivity ranking
published alongside). Track R ranked separately. Order is
PENDING-STAKEHOLDER elicitation (Appendix A v1 §8).

## 8. Extraction & scoring

**Schema.** All evidence enters through the extraction CSV
defined in `extraction-schema.md` (template:
`extraction_template.csv`). One row per claim (candidate ×
gate × window × stratum × source). Provenance = commit +
source ID (DOI/PMID/NCT/FDA doc) + page/table/section locator
(GOAL §9). Every research agent returns `complete`,
`evidence[]` (inspected full texts/data/code only — snippets
are pointers), `unresolved[]`, a structured claims table, and
gate scores in extraction-CSV form. Model + effort per agent
recorded in review/log artifacts (GOAL §9 rule 1).

**Human-led dual extraction (GOAL §9 — non-negotiable).**
Agents may *pre-extract*. Every load-bearing (gate-deciding)
claim then requires independent verification by a human
extractor **independent of the holder**, plus a sampled error
audit with the error rate reported. If the holder extracts a
load-bearing claim, it receives external audit instead.
Agent-only verification is banned for gate-deciding claims.
Status at this protocol's date: **PENDING** for all Step 0/1
rows (first human targets per Step 0 §5: Otarmeni USPI label
numbers by direct PDF re-read; Downie errata check; Kral
full texts; NEJM full text beyond abstract). The
`extractor` + `verified_date` CSV fields record who verified
what, when; an agent ID in `extractor` without a human
verifier entry keeps the row pre-extraction only.
Corrections are new versions (no-overwrite evidence rule).

**Scoring computation.** `research/scoring/gate_table.py`
(first locked computation, GOAL §7) reads the versioned
extraction CSV and emits the gate matrix + fitness ranking
deterministically. At this protocol's date `gate_table.py`
**does not yet exist** — writing it, with passing tests and a
pinned software environment, is a Step 2 completion item
(GOAL §11) tracked in `freeze-checklist.md`. No hand-scored
headline may substitute for the script once it exists;
`verify/` locks it (plus Appendix D arithmetic) once stable.
README "how to run a generation" is added *with* the script,
not before (GOAL §9).

**Skeptics.** Adversarial review with a distinct falsification
target per load-bearing claim (GOAL §7). Failed/unavailable
reviewer = synthesis note, never a vote. Skeptic downgrades
are applied as new versions; original worker wording is
preserved (Step 0/1 precedent).

## 9. Judges, blinding, dissent, human-expert roster

**LLM judges triage; human experts decide freezes**
(GOAL §9). LLM judge panels may rank, flag, and draft
verdicts for human adjudication. They may not: freeze a
baseline or candidate, ratify a convention threshold, unblock
G3b, or convert INSUFFICIENT to PASS. Judge *opinion* is not
evidence and never imputes a missing cell (§6).

**Judge eligibility (human freeze panel).** Before any
freeze-for-handoff: named reviewers covering the roster slots
below. Eligibility requires: (a) expertise matching the slot;
(b) disclosed conflicts (inventor/manufacturer/program ties
to any family A–H → recusal from that family's freeze vote;
allegiance disclosed even when not recusing); (c) no
authorship of a candidate under judgement in the same
generation (authors may answer questions, not vote);
(d) holder participates under §12 COI rules — sets budget,
never sole-scores, never sole-approves a (re-)freeze, and for
G3b co-design sits as one parent voice, never chair-with-veto
(Appendix A v1 §6). LLM judges: model + effort logged per
run; no LLM judge may adjudicate a claim it authored in the
same run without independent (human or separate-run xhigh)
review — self-adjudication is logged and discounted.

**Blinding.** Judges are blinded to candidate *authorship*
(which worker/agent/lab advocate drafted Appendix B) where
feasible (GOAL §9). Mechanism/family cannot be blinded (it is
the object of judgement) and is not pretended otherwise.
Unblinding events are logged. Extractors verifying a claim
are blinded to the proposing worker's verdict
(`verdict_proposed`) until their independent values are
recorded, where the workflow permits.

**Adjudication & dissent.** Freeze/promotion decisions:
majority of the eligible human panel **with written dissent
preserved verbatim** in the decision record (GOAL §9). A
dissent on a G4/G5 veto or G3b matter blocks promotion until
answered in writing (answer recorded; dissent never deleted).
Tie or missing quorum (any roster slot empty) = no freeze —
deliverables stay labelled open synthesis (GOAL §5.1).
Adoption by an external lab is a stretch outcome with its
own criterion (named group commits to run/fund a handoff
design); incumbent engagement is never assumed.
