## 2. Mechanism & target cells

Target cells: auditory-nerve axons (SGN central processes). Explicitly **not** ABI (cochlear-nucleus surface array; different class, unverified here). Prior art owner concept: Lenarz et al., *Bioelectron Med* 2025;11:26, PMID 41131644 (Step 0 D2a: concept/review, human use conditional/future; no N or bench numbers in abstract).
