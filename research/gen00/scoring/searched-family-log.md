## 1. What counts as one cell (counting method — read first)

Protocol §7 defines the searched family as *every candidate
× gate × window × stratum scored, including all INSUFFICIENT
cells*. Formal family-wise control (Holm / hierarchical
gatekeeping, stated before running) applies **only** to
quantitative meta-analytic sub-claims with pre-listed primary
comparisons; qualitative gate scores use this log to discount
narrative confidence, labelled as such. This file therefore
counts at two levels and never merges them silently:

- **Level 1 — gate-window cells.** One cell = candidate ×
  Appendix A §2 gate-cell (`G1-spectral`, `G1-temporal`,
  `G2`, `G2-music`, `G3a`, `G3b`, `G4` incl. `G4-AAV`,
  `G5`, `G6`) × window. Windows use the extraction-schema
  values `W0` (0–9 mo), `W1` (9–24 mo), `W2` (2–3.5 y),
  `W3` (3.5–7 y), `ADOL` (adolescence sensitivity), `ALL`
  (pre-declared window-spanning), `NA` (pre-declared NA).
  A plan row written "W1–W3" counts as 3 cells; "all +
  adolescence" counts as `ALL` + `ADOL` = 2 cells; "W0–W1
  mechanism only" still counts (2 cells) — mechanism-only
  cells are scored cells, not free cells. `G1` windows are
  the child-age claim the proxy evidence is asked to
  support, not the age of tested listeners (Appendix A §2
  fn.; candidate A §7 fn.).
- **Level 2 — stratum-expanded cells.** Where a measurement
  plan requires a *separate* verdict per stratum (G3a per
  modality; G4 per cochlear segment / per component; G5 per
  Appendix C harm; G6 per coverage/equity subgroup; Family G
  per decision origin × genotype state; Q0-P per
  time-since-implant band), §3 gives the multiplier and the
  expanded count. Crossed multipliers are **not** multiplied
  into one headline number unless the plan pre-registers the
  full cross — most plans require marginal (one-dimension-
  at-a-time) reporting, so §3 reports each dimension
  separately. Any future analysis that crosses dimensions
  (e.g. modality × etiology × window) enlarges the family
  and must be appended here by dated amendment **before**
  its results are reported.
- **Sensitivity re-reads are additional looks, logged in
  §4, not hidden inside Level 1/2:** G1-spectral anchors
  (4/8/10/16), Q0-P/Q0-A alternative cuts, evolving-standard
  (sensitivity) comparator re-reads, per-protocol /
  matched-intensity re-reads, hierarchical-gatekeeping
  order swaps. A verdict reported at four anchors is four
  looks at one cell, not four cells — but it is four
  opportunities to select a favourable anchor, which is
  exactly what this log exists to make visible.

**Comparator note.** The deciding comparator for every cell
is `frozen_bilateral_eCI_v1` (concurrently measured where the
plan requires a delta). The evolving standard is a
sensitivity comparator only; beating frozen but not evolving
= implementation gap, never a C0 win. Both looks are logged
(§4).

## 3. Level-2 stratum expansion (the family Step 3 actually searches)

### 3.6 Q0-P × time band × cohort; Q0-A × readout

Q0-P: patency n/N (%) per **time-since-implant band (as
reported, never pooled)** × cohort definition (age at
implant, array type/era, round-window vs cochleostomy,
access definition/grader). Number of bands is source-driven
— every band reported is a cell; the primary read is the
longest band at ≥Moderate GRADE. Denominators are
treatment-policy (explanted/failed/ungradeable stay in N).
Q0-A: **2 co-readouts** (A1 ratio, A2 dB difference) per
study; zone needs both; single-readout studies cap at
PARTIAL-eligible. Minimum evidence for any
