## Preserved dissents (verbatim for the human panel)

**J2-Diss-1 — A G2 W3 adult nulls: relabel, do not count as
"signals".** I dissent from retaining PROVISIONAL-SIGNAL as the
row label for the three adult group-null studies and from the
skeptic's D8 shorthand "A 4 (1 mechanism + 3 nulls)" if quoted
as "4 signals". A null without an interval estimate against the
clinical comparator, at abstract level, in adults, is not a
signal of pediatric benefit *or* harm; calling it one invites
the next reader to count evidence *volume* where the studies
certify only that benefit was not demonstrated under those
conditions. Recommended label: `PROVISIONAL-NULL (adult T3)` at
row level, INSUFFICIENT for W3-child claims, tallied in
synthesis as "A: 1 mechanism study + 3 adult null studies",
never "4 signals". I agree with scorer and skeptic that these
rows cannot FAIL G2 (no ≥Moderate, same-test NI breach) and
that the gate stays INSUFFICIENT. This dissent changes
nomenclature and counting only.

**J2-Diss-2 — F G2 W2 P1 row (PMID 42194183): downgrade row to
INSUFFICIENT-for-F-effect.** The scorer keeps a
PROVISIONAL-SIGNAL row (collapsing to INSUFFICIENT at gate);
the skeptic (D7) forbids its use as F1 audibility/time-to-CI
evidence but leaves it "provisional-by-cap". For consistency
with D5 (H ASL-CDI → INSUFFICIENT-for-effect), I would
downgrade the row itself. Reasons: (i) non-significant P1
latency differences (p = 1.00/0.55/0.48) in a retrospective
sample are absence-of-demonstrated-difference, not equivalence
— no equivalence margin or interval is reported; (ii) P1
latency indexes cortical detection/maturation and is
insensitive to the spectral resolution on which speech
perception and language depend, with no validated mediation to
W2/W3 language in this record; (iii) the HA arm is selected on
achieving 30–50 dB aided thresholds, which selects on residual
cochlear substrate — precisely the F1 precondition (audibility
at ≥70 dB often insufficient) the study therefore cannot
test; (iv) comparator is unilateral CI, fitting ≈27 mo (W2,
not W0), decision-time/treatment-policy analysis absent. What
remains — "adequate early audibility, when achieved, tracks
cortical maturation" — is background mechanism consistency
already carried by Sharma, not an F signal. Gate verdict
unchanged (INSUFFICIENT); D7's use-prohibition upheld and
strengthened.

**J2-Diss-3 — C G1: "one programme, two preparations", not
literally one signal.** I agree C counts as a single
inventor-ecosystem (Moser lineage) line of evidence, attracts
the allegiance downgrade, needs non-inventor replication, and
cannot PASS. I dissent only from phrasing that erases the
cross-species step: acute gerbil tonotopy (PMID 32596983) plus
rodent+NHP thresholds (PMID 40721511) is modestly more than the
same result twice — it is mechanism consistency across
preparations within one programme. This does not raise GRADE,
does not create independence, and does not change any cell;
synthesis should write "one inventor programme (gerbil + NHP
preparations)", never "two studies" and never "replicated".
