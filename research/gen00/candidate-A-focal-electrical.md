## 3. Why prior focusing failed to translate — the explanation this candidate must beat (§8A requirement)

Step 0 corrected “without durable translation” to **mixed: acute positive,
take-home null** (Ch2b). Any new focusing claim must explain all of:

1. **Physics/charge cost.** Phased-array/tripolar focusing narrows
   spread of excitation only “at the cost of increased total stimulation
   current” (van den Honert & Kelsall 2007, Step 0 Ch2a, PMID 17552721);
   dynamic focusing cost −48% battery life (5.1 h, p<0.001) vs monopolar
   (van Groesen 2023, PMID 36279119). Narrower spread on a bench metric
   therefore trades directly against charge safety headroom, battery life,
   and loudness growth — candidate must show spread reduction *at safe
   charge in a fibrotic/growing-cochlea model* (kill criterion §11), with
   fibrotic tissue shunting explicitly modelled (human post-CI fibrous
   tissue + new bone in 17/17 archival bones, Kamakura & Nadol 2016,
   PMID 27371868; granulomatous reaction 27/28, Seyyedi & Nadol 2014,
   PMID 25122600 — adult, older-device series; Step 0 E4a).
2. **Degraded-comparator artifact.** The clearest acute win — Srinivasan
   2013 partial-tripolar +2.7 dB (digits) / +3 dB (sentences), PMID
   23467170 — was against a *degraded experimental monopolar* map, with no
   acute deficit vs the clinical map; N not in abstract. AppendixA-v1 §3.2
   therefore forbids PASS vs vocoder/degraded comparators. This candidate
   pre-commits to the listener's/child's **clinical monopolar map on the
   named frozen array** as comparator.
3. **Take-home nulls / adaptation & heterogeneity.** van Groesen 2023
   take-home crossover (13 fitted, 9 completed): null in noise (“does not
   consistently improve”); Bierer & Litvak 2016 (9 ears/8 listeners): group
   null, poorer performers tended better; Caswell-Midwinter & Arenberg
   2023 (14 ears): group null. Candidate must pre-specify (i) a take-home /
   chronic endpoint, not acute-only; (ii) a heterogeneity analysis
   (electrode–modiolus distance, neural-health proxy, baseline performance)
   with subgroup claims pre-listed under multiplicity control (§5.2) — a
   post-hoc “poor performers benefit” claim scores INSUFFICIENT.
4. **Channels-vs-maxima confusion.** Berg 2019 (PMID 31067952, Step 0 Ch1b):
   16-channel/16-maxima beat 8 in imaging-verified perimodiolar recipients,
   benefit tracking electrode–modiolus distance. Modern frozen arrays may
   already exceed the 2001 Friesen plateau (~7–8, PMID 11519582, Ch1a —
   correct authors Friesen, Shannon, Baskent & Wang). Anatomy fitting is
   thus partly *already frozen/evolving practice*; the challenger delta
   must be isolated (focusing on/off × anatomy-fitting on/off factorial or
   equivalent pre-stated design), or the G1 win belongs to the array, not
   to this candidate.
5. **Clinical adoption unverified.** No FDA-label/CTgov adoption record for
   focusing was inspected in Step 0 (Ch2b). “No durable adoption” must not
   be asserted as a market fact until that search exists (Step 0 C5).

*Net hypothesis (to test, not premise):* focusing failed because spread
reduction was bought with charge/battery, tested acutely against weakened
comparators, and diluted across unselected anatomies/neural substrates —
not because spectral resolution is irrelevant (NH vocoder gains to ≥20
channels, Ch1a). Each element is falsifiable in §7.
