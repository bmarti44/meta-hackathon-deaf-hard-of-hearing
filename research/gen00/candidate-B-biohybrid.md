(G1) without giving up Track P preservation (G4 veto applies in full).

## 3. Tonotopy, lifetime/refill, and the 2014–2026 silence (§8B pre-specifications)

**Tonotopy metrics (all pre-declared; growth alone never passes):**
(a) place-pitch / characteristic-frequency order: rank correlation between
contact position (mm from round window, imaging-verified) and neural
best-frequency / behavioural place-pitch rank, reported pre- vs
post-regrowth in the same animals/ears; (b) spread of excitation (mm and
octaves, AppA G1) pre/post regrowth vs frozen-array control; (c) ectopic
branching rate: proportion of traced neurites terminating outside the
target contact's tonotopic zone (zone width pre-stated); (d) SGN survival
density by cochlear segment. **PASS direction:** order preserved or
improved *and* spread narrowed; growth with degraded order is the §11 kill,
not a partial win. No metric threshold number is invented here — numeric
tonotopy PASS margins require same-model frozen distributions
(UNPINNABLE today) and must be frozen before T2 data are seen (§7 rule 7).

**Lifetime / refill burden plan (mandatory, scored in G5/G6):** state in
years: elution/expression duration with source tier; number of refill,
replacement, or revision surgeries **from implantation to adolescence**
(counting rule §10); behaviour after depletion (benefit persistence vs
neurite retraction — measured, not assumed); growth interaction (infant
cochlea/skull growth vs reservoir/array position). **GOAL §8B automatic
kill: a <5 y reservoir without refill.** A plan whose duration rests only
on Pinyon's short-deafness guinea-pig expression window = INSUFFICIENT
