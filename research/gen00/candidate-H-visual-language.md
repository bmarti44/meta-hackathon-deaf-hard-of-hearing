## 5. Model system / comparator + identification strategy (binding)
**Design:** prospective **encouragement design** nested in observational cohort. Families are randomized (or quasi-randomized by pre-stated rollout) to **encouragement** = enhanced offer of signed-language EI (funded Deaf-mentor access, scheduling/transport support, information) vs frozen EI referral — **never to modality itself**; every family remains free to choose signed/spoken/bimodal and to decline. **Identification:** (1) primary = intent-to-treat effect of encouragement (policy-relevant, unconfounded by randomization); (2) secondary = complier-average effect of signed-exposure dose using encouragement as instrument, with exclusion/monotonicity assumptions stated and falsification checks (encouragement must not affect outcomes except via exposure/EI contact — contact-time-matched attention control for spoken-EI support pre-specified to defend exclusion); (3) observational dose-response with S4.2 adjustment set (home language, SES proxy, EI dose, additional disabilities, etiology, CI age) labelled non-causal sensitivity. **Adjustment alone never claims causality (S4.2/AppA-v1 S5.1).** Negative-control outcome + pre-trends (where available) pre-registered. Comparator: frozen EI mix exactly as pinned P07 (family-chosen modality as actually received, not assumed sign-free) — H must beat that mix on dose/timing or vanish into evolving standard. ROBINS-I for non-randomized components; GRADE per S5.2.
