## 1. What counts as "outside A–H" (boundary fences)

A focal electrical · B biohybrid eluting · C optical ·
D reprogramming / beyond-*OTOF* AAV (long-horizon, removed
from infant promotion; single advance criterion =
replicated threshold restoration with cochlear-identity
synapsed cells in mature deafened large animal) ·
E penetrating ANI/ABI · F W0 bridge · G sequencing/triage
pathway · H visual language. A mechanism below is "new"
only where its *mechanism and kill question* differ from
A–H; overlaps are fenced per row (§5). Anything already in
clinical trials is an external program — per §2.2 it trends
to *evolving standard* (comparator), never this project's
invention. That rule bites hardest on robotics/ECochG (§4).

supporting-cell fate conversion; D's advance criterion is

### 2.3 ASO / splice modulation

PMID **23380860** (Nat Med 2013): single systemic ASO dose
in neonatal *Ush1c* c.216G>A mice partially corrected
splicing, rescued low-frequency hearing + vestibular
function for several months; PMID **28633508** (2017):
vestibular rescue only in a P1–P5 critical window, minimal
at P15. Tier T1, Ns not in abstracts. Bounded
ClinicalTrials.gov search found **no cochlear ASO trial**;
the human *USH2A* ASO (ultevursen/QR-421a) records
(NCT03780257 completed; NCT05158296/NCT05176717 terminated)
are **retinal** (intravitreal, retinitis pigmentosa), not
hearing interventions. **Infant relevance:** reversible,
re-dosable, non-AAV (escapes G4-AAV) — attractive
properties; but evidence is one variant, one mouse model,
a neonatal window that maps poorly to human decision
time, and repeat intracochlear/systemic dosing in infants
is untested anywhere in inspected records.

### 2.4 Cell therapy — otic neural progenitors

PMID **22972191** (Nature 2012): human ESC-derived otic
neuroprogenitors transplanted in an auditory-neuropathy
animal model engrafted and significantly improved
auditory-evoked thresholds (T2-class animal model; N not
in abstract). Human record: **NCT07032038** (Rincell-1,
Rinri): Phase I/IIa randomized open-label FIH in **adults**
with neural hearing loss eligible for unilateral CI —
CI+Rincell-1 vs CI alone, 2 cohorts × 10 planned (12 dosed
planned), status **NOT_YET_RECRUITING** (verified 2026-09;
est. start 2027-09 in the record as inspected — press
pointers suggesting earlier starts were not used as
evidence). **Zero humans dosed in the inspected record.**
Infant relevance: targets the ANSD/neural stratum that CI
serves worst, and is designed as a CI *adjunct* (does not
compete with FROZEN) — but tumorigenicity, engraftment
control, and any immunosuppression in infants are
unaddressed at T4, and congenital P0 is not the trial
population (presbycusis + adult auditory neuropathy).

### 2.6 AI/ML sound processing + beamforming for CI

**Mechanism.** DNN source separation / denoising *before*
(or as) the sound-coding strategy — attacks multi-talker
noise, which single-microphone stationary-noise reduction
and conventional beamforming do not. Distinct from A
(current focusing changes *where* charge goes; this
changes *what signal* is encoded).

| Record | Design / N | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| PMID **38853168** (Sci Rep 2024): RNN + SepFormer separation | Tested in **13 CI listeners** (T3, adult) | Both DNNs significantly improved speech intelligibility in stationary *and* multi-talker noise without overall quality loss (effect sizes not in abstract) |
| PMID **37030808** (IEEE TBME 2023): "Deep ACE" end-to-end denoising electrodogram | Listening tests, **8 CI users** (T3) + objective measures | Highest intelligibility vs baseline algorithms; designed for no added latency |

**Infant relevance.** Highest near-term infant relevance
in this sweep: software on the FROZEN device — no new
surgery, no biology touched, reversible, updatable. But:
(i) all inspected tests are adult, postlingual, lab-based,
single-session — **no pediatric, no W2/W3 language, no
take-home durability record**; (ii) real-time on-processor
compute/power at infant wear-time is unproven in inspected
records; (iii) G2's bar is ≥3 dB SRT (App. A) in W3
children — no inspected abstract reports a dB SRT effect
extractable against that bar. Partial evolving-standard
boundary: scene classification and DNN noise reduction
already ship in hearing aids / processor firmware
(pointer-level knowledge, no inspected CI-processor record
in this sweep) — any Gen 5 candidate must benchmark
against the *current commercial processor*, not 2023 lab
baselines, or it wins by comparator drift.

### 2.7 Electro-acoustic stimulation (EAS) advances

PMID **39815407** (Ear Hear 2025): prospective 12-mo trial,
**24 children aged 5–17 y** with functional low-frequency
hearing; EAS users scored better on prosody +
subjective hearing than electric-only. PMID **37167445**
(2023): retrospective **40 children (48 ears)**, LF PTA
≤75 dB HL; <half of EAS-eligible children chose EAS; **no
speech-perception difference** (words, sentences,
sentences-in-noise) for preservation or EAS use. Tier T4
but school-age, residual-hearing subgroup — **not P0**:
P0 requires better-ear ≥70 dB averaged 0.5–4 kHz, so most
of P0 has no aidable low-frequency hearing to preserve.
EAS serves a residual-hearing entry-time stratum at the
P0 edge, and hearing-preservation surgery is already the
§2.2 evolving standard / A-comparator.

### 2.8 Totally / fully implantable CI (TICI)

PMID **39779916** (Commun Med 2025; NCT04571333):
non-randomized feasibility, **6 adults** with bilateral
severe–profound SNHL, 52 wk; 1 anticipated serious adverse
device effect (resolved); speech in quiet/noise
**comparable** (not superior) to external processor;
satisfaction/usage high. T3. No G1/G2 superiority theory —
the claim would be wear-time/burden (G6) and autonomy
(G3b, BLOCKED). Infant-specific costs are severe on their
face: internal battery finite life ⇒ replacement surgeries
across a childhood (G5/G6), skull growth, microphone under
skin, no easy processor upgrades — none addressed for
children in inspected records. Adult pivotal programs
exist at pointer level (NCT07139327 et al., not inspected
as full records here).

### 2.9 Middle-ear implants / bone-conduction devices

PMID **20610910** (inspected): active middle-ear implants
indicated for **moderate-to-severe** loss; mixed-loss cases
cited have **30–60 dB HL** sensorineural components. Bone
conduction by mechanism must drive a cochlea with
serviceable bone-conduction thresholds (cf. F's
precondition, §8F: bone conduction cannot overcome
≥70 dB SNHL audibility). P0 primary analysis already
*excludes* conductive/mixed loss with normal bone
conduction (§3.1) — the population these devices serve.
**Population mismatch, not an evidence gap.**

### 2.10 Robotics / ECochG-guided insertion; vestibular-sparing arrays

PMID **34600798** (Hear Res 2022): single-blind RCT,
**60 adults** — ECochG-triggered electrode withdrawal on
≥30% cochlear-microphonic drop significantly improved
hearing preservation at 3 mo (median absolute loss 20 dB
intervention vs 30 dB control, p=.013). PMID **38206061**
(2024): prospective single-arm robotics-assisted insertion
+ concurrent ECochG, 21 adults / 22 ears with residual
hearing — feasible, stable PTAs to 1 y. Both T3, adults
with residual hearing (again the P0-edge stratum, not the
≥70 dB core). **Classification is settled by GOAL itself:**
§8A names ECochG-guided / robotic insertion and
dexamethasone-eluting arrays as *evolving-standard
comparator elements, not challenger novelty*; §2.2 lists
hearing-preservation CI (incl. these trials) as evolving
standard. Vestibular-sparing (atraumatic arrays, insertion
speed/depth control) is the same surgical-technique class.
**Therefore: not a new family.** Its correct Gen 5 use is
input to a human comparator re-freeze decision
(`frozen_bilateral_eCI_v2`?) under §3.4 — holder +
independent co-approver only; this sweep does not re-freeze
anything. Note the claim cap: any future candidate beating
FROZEN-v1 only because FROZEN lacks ECochG/robotics has an
*implementation gap*, never a C0 win (§3.4).

## 3. Proposed new Families I / J / K (strongest three)

Ranking logic (stated, falsifiable): inspected-evidence
strength for congenital severe–profound SNHL × infant
decision-time relevance × a satisfiable-on-paper gate path
under the unchanged PASS bar. All three are **proposals
for Gen 5 pre-registration**, not candidates, not scores.

### Family I — Non-*OTOF* AAV gene replacement, *GJB2*-anchored
(*STRC*, *MYO7A* as pipeline variants behind the anchor)

- **Why strongest #1:** the only swept mechanism with a
  recruiting *pediatric* human trial in a core P0 stratum
  (NCT07627971, 9 mo–7 y, ≥85 dB), two independent 2025
  mouse rescues with cell-specificity solved as the stated
  mechanism (PMIDs 41402290, 40121530), and NHP/pig
  delivery-safety records. Largest single-gene stratum in
  §3.2 by name (fraction unpinned).
- **Track proposal:** product = **Track R** (AAV ⇒ G4-AAV
  presumptive FAIL bars Track-P promotion; infant
  promotion impossible until human data). The scoreable
  Gen 5 candidate is a **Track-P pathway component via
  Family G** (G1 = NA pre-declared): decision-time triage
  extended from *OTOF*-only to *OTOF*+*GJB2* eligibility as
  registry results land — it inherits G's kill (no
  time-to-access / option-preservation gain vs frozen
  workup ⇒ kill) and QR4's G4-AAV legs.
- **Kill/advance (proposed):** retire the anchor if SONIX
  (or successor) reports no ABR-threshold signal at its
  primary completion, or a G5 signal (e.g. loss of CI
  candidacy in treated ears) at ≥Moderate GRADE.

### Family J — AI/ML front-end processing for CI
(DNN source separation / deep-denoising sound coding)

- **Why strongest #2:** only swept mechanism that is
  simultaneously (a) human-tested in actual CI users with
  significant intelligibility gains (PMIDs 38853168,
  37030808), (b) biology-untouched and reversible, and
  (c) deployable to the *entire* P0 CI population,
  genotype-agnostic — the coverage profile every molecular
  family lacks.
- **Track proposal: Track P** — with a named protocol
  problem humans must rule on **before** scoring: J does
  not increase effective channels, so as a "device" it
  fails G1 by construction. Its honest claim is G2
  (≥3 dB SRT-in-noise, W3) with G4/G5 preserved trivially
  and G1 = inherited-frozen. Pre-register as **G1 = NA
  (portfolio component, F/G/H-style)** or do not field it;
  inventing a channel claim for a denoiser would repeat
  v1's deleted "equivalent functional gain" escape hatch
  (§4.2). This ruling is a HOLDER-PIN, not made here.
- **Kill (proposed):** no ≥3 dB SRT advantage over the
  *current commercial processor* (not lab baselines) in
  W3-age children, or latency/power forcing wear-time loss
  vs frozen (G6 harm), ⇒ kill.

### Family K — In-vivo precision editing
(base editing lead; prime editing + ASO as watched sub-arms)

- **Why strongest #3:** repeated, multi-gene, multi-lab
  neonatal-mouse rescues in 2025 alone
  (*POU4F3* PMID 40968144 near-complete ≥4 mo; *MPZL2*
  PMID 40764318 — both functional hearing; *GJB2*-R75W
  PMID 40059830 — structural (gap-junction) restoration,
  no threshold claim in abstract), mechanistically
  distinct from both replacement (I) and reprogramming
  (D): endogenous-locus correction, expression under
  native regulation — the property whose absence
  (ectopic expression) is I's stated ototoxicity risk.
- **Track proposal: Track R, long-horizon watch** (like
  D): scored for learning; infant promotion barred until
  adult human feasibility (§10) — of which there is none;
  the only human-ward editing record is withdrawn at N=0
  (NCT06025032). Prime editing enters **only** as a T0
  watch sub-arm (PMIDs 42562992, 39842828 — in-vitro);
  ASO (§2.3) as the non-AAV RNA-level watch sub-arm.
  Neither gets its own family on current evidence.
- **Advance criterion (proposed, D-style):** replicated
  *durable* (≥6 mo) threshold restoration after treatment
  of a **mature/deafened** animal (not P1 neonate) with
  genome-wide off-target assessment — the Baringo
  transient-rescue result (PMID 32493795) is the failure
  mode this criterion exists to exclude.

## 5. Fences vs the Gen 0–4 record (nothing is reopened by this sweep)

- **I ≠ D:** replacement of a missing gene product ≠
  supporting-cell reprogramming. D's advance criterion,
  B's kill, E's park, C's watch: all unchanged. I's mouse
  rescues are **not** D-criterion evidence.
- **I needs G, does not replace it:** Gen4 §6 portfolio
  (P4/W4/QR4) remains the decision-time machinery into
  which I inserts one new eligibility stratum. QR4's
  G4-AAV presumption applies to I in every leg; Q0 stays
  UNDECIDABLE-no-data. NCT07627971 excludes bilateral CI
  (inspected eligibility) — a *label/trial* fact listed
  separately from biology, per §1.3.
- **J ≠ A:** A1's kill stays UNEVALUABLE; J claims no
  spread reduction and inherits no A evidence. J must
  explain nothing about focusing — and gains nothing if
  focusing ever works.
- **No fitness claim:** I/J/K are unscored. Fielding any
  of them requires the Gen 5 equivalents of protocol,
  held-out manifest, extraction schema, and human
  ratification of the two named rulings (J's G1 status;
  I's G-interface) — plus the standing W1–W8 human acts.
  **G3b BLOCKED. FROZEN retained. No PASS anywhere.**
