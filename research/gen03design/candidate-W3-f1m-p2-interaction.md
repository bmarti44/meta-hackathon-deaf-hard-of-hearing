## 2. NEW falsification question + anti-rewording audit + wild-card defence

- **Parents' questions.** F1m: vs concurrently measured
  frozen HA trial, does W0 optimisation increase audibility
  dose *and/or* shorten time-to-CI decision (kill = both
  null, or W2 null/harm)? P2: is attributable triage delay
  exactly zero while gene-first capture is retained? Both
  are **main-effect** questions about averages/processes.
  Every Gen 0–2 pathway file then *narrates* the two as
  composing additively — better W0 access plus better
  sequencing equals better pathway — an assumption no
  estimand in three generations has ever put at risk.
- **W3's question (new — an interaction, not a sum).** Does
  achieved W0 audibility dose **modify** the pathway effect?
  Pre-stated form: within P2's wedge, stratify children by
  pre-registered W0 audibility-dose band (aided SII +
  datalogged wear, measured before CI decision — F1m's
  estimand 1 used as a *baseline modifier*, bands frozen
  pre-enrolment) and estimate the pathway-vs-frozen
  contrast on (i) time-to-CI-decision/activation and
  (ii) W2/W3 G2/G3a-spoken endpoints **per band, with the
  band×pathway interaction as the primary estimand**.
  Falsifiable in three directions, each with different
  consequences no parent can produce: **(a) benefit
  concentrated** — pathway gain appears only in the low-
  audibility band (triage compensates for poor aided
  access) ⇒ the pooled P2 claim must narrow to that band
  and F1m optimisation becomes a *substitute* for part of
  the sequencing apparatus in the high band; **(b) harm
  concentrated** — attributable/process costs or downstream
  nulls sit in one band (e.g. high-audibility children,
  doing well aided, are the ones a gene-first detour can
  only slow) ⇒ band-restricted kill (S12) that pooled I1\*
  zero-tolerance alone would mask on the distributional
  leg; **(c) no interaction** within pre-stated precision ⇒
  the additivity story three generations assumed is, for
  the first time, *evidence* instead of narration, and
  F1m+P2 may be scored as separable components thereafter.
  A reworded F1m ("does optimisation help?") or P2 ("does
  triage delay?") cannot state, let alone answer, "for
  whom does the combination change sign?"
- **Why this is not the rejected Gen 2 axis.** Gen 2
  rejected **A1×F1m** (W0 dose × focal-vs-monopolar post-CI
  benefit) because its experiment cannot execute until
  A1's UNEVALUABLE T0 kill clears (still true; GEN3-AD3).
  W3's interface experiment needs no A1 verdict: both
  components run on marketed/approved care inside one T4
  protocol, and its modifier (audibility dose) is measured
  by F1m's own existing estimand. The letter Gen 2 found
  satisfiable ("an interface experiment *can* be stated")
  is here also *executable* — the defect that killed the
  earlier axis is absent.
- **Rejected own draft (rewording, not filed):** "P2 with
  F1m's fitting targets added to its W0 checklist." A
